Management report – 17 Nov 2026

Summary. 206 leads came in, all assigned to a branch. 165 (80%) have been worked and 41 (20%) are still at "Lead" with no activity note. AP / TS delivered 123 leads (60% of the day), Tamil Nadu 44, Karnataka 30 and Kerala 9. No leads for Indore.

Insights

  • Untouched leads: Kakinada (10/37) and Coimbatore (9/18) hold 19 of the 41; Tamil Nadu has 16 and Karnataka 7 (Bangalore 4/7). Chennai, Nellore and Vizag have cleared almost everything.
  • Call Back is the largest stage (76, 37%). Notes are mostly RNR / no response / number busy, so these are unreached rather than qualified. Nellore (21), Kakinada (14), Chennai (12) and Vizag (12) carry the most.
  • Real interest is small: 55 leads in Follow Up / Warm (8 warm). Follow-up notes show CV requests and branch-visit intent, mainly from Vizag (12), Kakinada (9) and Nellore (8).
  • Cold and dead: 34 leads. Mysore has 4 of its 9 leads dead (dialed by mistake, not enquired), and Vizag has 6 of 37: worth a lead-quality check on both.
  • Volume is concentrated. Vizag, Nellore and Kakinada took 113 of 206 leads (55%). Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Salem, Madhurai, Bangalore, Kochi, Thrissur and Mangalore each got 11 or fewer.

Actions for tomorrow

  • Clear all 41 untouched leads first, starting with Kakinada and Coimbatore, with a first-call and stage update before noon.
  • Retry RNR / Call Back leads at a different time of day plus WhatsApp, and move them to Cold only after the agreed attempts.
  • Prioritise the 8 warm and 47 follow-up leads: confirm CV receipt and branch-visit dates.
  • Review the Mysore dead-lead reasons with the campaign team and tighten targeting or form questions.
