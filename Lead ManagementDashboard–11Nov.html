Report: insights

  • Zone view: AP / TS brought 185 leads (71%) and Tamil Nadu 56 (22%), against 17 for Karnataka and 2 for Kerala and Indore combined. Tamil Nadu has the highest no-contact share at 50% versus 44% for AP / TS, so Tamil Nadu callbacks need the first push tomorrow.
  • Volume is concentrated in six branches. Vijayawada (41), Vishakhapatnam (39), Kakinada (38), Nellore (34), Hyderabad (31) and Chennai (28) hold 211 of 260 leads (81%). Karnataka branches got very little: Hubli 9, Mangalore 3, Bangalore 3, Mysore 2.
  • Half the day is still sitting in Call Back. 132 leads (51%) are in Call Back, and for 102 of them the notes only record unanswered calls (RNR, call not answered, no response).
  • Vijayawada, Chennai and Vishakhapatnam have the weakest contact rate. About 63%, 75% and 49% of their leads show no conversation yet. Hyderabad is the best of the large branches at 19%, with 14 engaged leads and 4 of the 7 Warm leads.
  • Follow Up is where real intent sits. Of 58 Follow Up leads, 40 have notes showing a concrete next step: CV or resume requested, branch visit planned, or fees discussed. These are the leads closest to conversion.
  • Kakinada has the highest drop-off. 7 of its 38 leads are not interested (reasons: local jobs, remote or IT roles, cost) and 9 are Cold, so lead quality or expectation-setting needs a look there.
  • Notes quality is uneven. 8 leads have no notes, and six of them are still at the "Lead" stage with no action. Many notes are one-word shorthand (rnr, cb, na), which makes manager review hard.

Actions for tomorrow

  • Priority 1: Re-attempt all 116 no-contact leads at a different time of day, then send a WhatsApp message. Start with Tamil Nadu, then Vijayawada, Vishakhapatnam and Nellore in AP / TS.
  • Priority 2: Review the 66 engaged leads and the 58 Follow Up leads. Confirm each pending CV, branch visit or payment discussion has a dated next step.
  • Priority 3: Move the 6 untouched "Lead" stage leads and the 7 Warm leads (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Nellore) to a first call by midday.
  • Priority 4: Ask branches to log full notes (location, education, experience, country interest, next date) instead of shorthand.
  • Priority 5: Check Kakinada and Hubli lead quality with the campaign team, since both show high Cold and Not interested counts.
