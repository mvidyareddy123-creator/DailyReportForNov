Report for management

Headline. 111 leads came in on 6 Nov, only 29 (26%) had been worked; 82 (74%) were still at the "Lead" stage with no notes.

Where leads are stuck. Vishakhapatnam received the most leads (27) and left 22 unworked. Nellore left 11 of 15 and Chennai 13 of 14. By contrast, Thrissur worked 7 of 12 and Kottayam 4 of 6.

Quality of what was worked. Of the 29 worked leads, 11 are at Call Back, most of them "RNR", "no response", "call back requested" or an invalid number; one lead was an invalid contact and one an ISD number. Several Kerala notes show enquiries for a child, or for studies rather than work visas, which points to targeting and form-screening issues. Good notes exist (age, education, location, experience, CV requested), and the three walk-ins (Mysore hot, Hyderabad warm, Mangalore follow-up) show offline conversion is happening.

Actions for tomorrow

  1. First-call sweep of all 82 unworked leads before noon, starting with Vishakhapatnam, Nellore and Chennai; branch heads confirm completion by 1 pm.
  2. Call Back leads: set a fixed retry slot (same day evening and next morning) and use WhatsApp after two missed calls; flag invalid numbers for form-validation review.
  3. Add a "who is the enquiry for / work or study" screening question on the Kerala forms to cut non-fit enquiries.
  4. Follow up the hot and warm walk-in leads (Mysore, Hyderabad) within 24 hours, since both have price discussions on record.
  5. Ask advisors to keep notes at the standard of the best examples: location, qualification, experience, country interest, next step.
