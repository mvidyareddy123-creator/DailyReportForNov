Management report – insights

  • The day’s volume came in after hours. 191 of 231 leads (83%) are Facebook leads created between 8 pm and midnight, and only 1 of them had any action at the time of the data pull. This is the main reason 204 leads (88%) are still in “Lead” stage. They need first calls first thing on 3 Nov.
  • Daytime leads were handled reasonably. Of the 39 leads that did not come from a Facebook form (walk-ins, referrals, advisor-created), 26 were worked (67%). Thrissur (4/4), Vijayawada (5/5), Bangalore (3/3) and Hyderabad (4/6) did well.
  • Andhra Pradesh carries 41% of volume. AP received 94 leads, 86 of them Facebook. Vijayawada (35) and Kakinada (23) are the biggest queues, and Kakinada’s 23 leads had just 1 worked.
  • Karnataka is the second queue. Mysore received 27 leads, all in “Lead” stage and all with two advisors (Nandana M K 14, Shalini M S 13). Bangalore received 24, with 3 worked.
  • Call-back quality is weak. 13 leads are in Call Back, and 9 of them have notes only saying “not answered”, “RNR” or similar. Vijayawada alone has 5, and Ruchitha Naga Venkata logged “not answered” on 4 leads. A call attempt with no second-attempt time is not a follow-up.
  • Best note quality came from Thrissur, Hyderabad and Nellore. Notes on Follow Up leads there carry age, qualification, experience, location and the next step (CV requested, branch visit tomorrow). Cold and Call Back notes elsewhere are one or two words, so a manager cannot tell what happened.
  • Hot signals to protect: two Warm leads (Kochi walk-in with a France package explained; Bangalore visit with an existing client) and Follow Ups with a branch visit or CV pending (Thrissur, Hyderabad, Nellore).
  • Data hygiene: 8 leads have no branch tagged (Selvi K ×5, Jayasree Pasupula, Rihana P A, Roopika P). Selvi K holds 6 leads in total and none was worked.
