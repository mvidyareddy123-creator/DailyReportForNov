Report – key insights

  • 179 of 328 leads (55%) were not worked. Only 149 moved out of the “Lead” stage, so more than half of the day’s enquiries are still waiting for a first action.
  • Backlog is concentrated. Highest untouched share among branches with 15+ leads: Vijayawada 82%, Kakinada 76%, Bangalore 65%, Nellore 58%, Chennai 56%. Best: Vizag 4%, Mysore 15%. Vijayawada alone holds 46 untouched leads of 56.
  • 21 advisors worked nothing (92 leads in total). Those with 5+ leads and no stage change: Ruchitha Naga Venkata (26), Naveena Shanmugham (11), Selvi K (9), Supriya Kotthapalli (6), Abhishek Dangi (5), Pratiksha Bhati (5), Keerthi Sudha Gadam (5).
  • Mysore is the model branch – 35 of 41 leads worked (85%) and 14 in Follow Up/Warm. Replicate its call-and-update routine elsewhere.
  • Contactability is a problem. 63 leads carry a not-reachable note against 69 real conversations; 15 are invalid or not interested. Second-attempt calls and WhatsApp follow-up are needed.
  • Note quality is thin. 181 leads (55%) have no activity note. Where notes exist, many are one word (“na”, “RNR”) without budget, profile or next step.
  • 37 leads have no FB form (advisor/manual entries) and 13 have no sales group – tag them so branch reporting is complete.

Actions for tomorrow

  1. First thing tomorrow, clear the untouched “Lead” queue in Vijayawada, Kakinada, Bangalore, Coimbatore and Indore – call every lead once before 12 pm and move the stage.
  2. Managers to review advisors with zero stage updates today and redistribute their leads among advisors who are keeping up.
  3. Every “Call Back” must have a dated next action; every RNR gets a second call at a different time plus a WhatsApp message.
  4. Minimum note standard: age, education/experience, passport, budget, country interest, next step.
  5. Assign a sales group to all unassigned leads and confirm the owner is the right branch advisor.
